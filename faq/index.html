Zakat guide & FAQ

Zakat is 2.5% of the wealth you have held for one lunar year, once it reaches the nisab threshold. ZakatDay does the arithmetic and keeps every figure on your device.

The basics

What is Zakat and who has to pay it? expand_more

Zakat is the obligatory annual charity in Islam: 2.5% of qualifying wealth held for one lunar year (hawl). It applies to adult Muslims who own wealth above the nisab threshold free of debt.

How is the nisab calculated? expand_more

Nisab is the minimum wealth at which Zakat becomes due. There are two classical measures: 87.48 g of gold or 612.36 g of silver. ZakatDay multiplies the current price per gram of the metal you choose by that weight.

Because silver is far cheaper, the silver nisab is much lower and is the standard most scholars recommend for cash-based wealth — it brings more people into Zakat and benefits the poor. Switch to the gold standard on the calculator if that is your madhhab's practice.

Which assets are included? expand_more
  • Cash at home, in bank accounts and in digital wallets
  • Gold and silver in any form — jewellery, coins or bars
  • Shares, funds, crypto and accessible pensions
  • Business stock held for sale and receivables likely to be repaid

Assets used personally — your home, car, furniture, tools of trade — are not zakatable.

How are debts treated? expand_more

Debts that must be paid now are subtracted from your assets before comparing against nisab. For long-term debts such as a mortgage, many scholars allow deducting only the instalments due in the coming year. Enter the figure your scholar advises.

Gold & silver

Why do karat and weight both matter? expand_more

Market prices quote fine (24K) gold. A 22K ring weighing 10 g contains 9.17 g of pure gold, so its zakatable value is 9.17 g × the 24K price. ZakatDay converts grams, tola and troy ounces to fine metal for you, then multiplies by the live price per gram.

  • 24K = 999 purity, 22K = 916, 21K = 875, 18K = 750, 14K = 585
  • 1 tola = 11.6638 g, 1 troy ounce = 31.1034768 g
Is jewellery worn daily still zakatable? expand_more

The schools differ: the Hanafi position is that gold and silver jewellery is zakatable regardless of use, while other schools exempt jewellery in regular wear. ZakatDay treats all entered metal as zakatable — follow your school.

Where do the live prices come from? expand_more

Currency and metal rates come from public feeds (jsDelivr's currency-api, with open.er-api.com as a fallback). The app converts the USD spot price per troy ounce into your currency per gram.

Retail prices in your city usually differ because of making charges and local premiums — use Edit on the calculator to enter the price you are actually quoted.

Privacy

What does ZakatDay store? expand_more

Nothing you type is stored. Amounts live in the page's memory and disappear when you close the tab. There is no account, no server database and no cookie carrying your figures.

Only four simple preferences are kept in your browser's local storage:

  • theme — light or dark
  • zakatday-currency — your chosen currency
  • zakatday-favorites — up to six pinned currencies
  • zakatday-nisab-standard — gold or silver nisab

These contain no money, no names and nothing that identifies you. Clearing site data removes them.

Does anything get sent over the network? expand_more

The page fetches exchange and metal prices, loads fonts from Google Fonts and records an anonymous page view with Google Analytics. Your amounts are never part of any of those requests, and no analytics event includes them.

What happens when I share my summary? expand_more

Sharing is entirely in your hands: ZakatDay copies the text to your clipboard or opens WhatsApp on your device. “Send to WhatsApp” builds a PNG receipt of your figures inside this tab, saves or hands it to your phone's share sheet, then opens WhatsApp with the text summary. Nothing is uploaded, stored on a server or added to your browser history.

You choose what the receipt contains. “Include itemized breakdown” lists each asset figure; “Total Zakat & Nisab only” hides every amount except the nisab threshold and the Zakat due. The choice lives in memory for the current tab only.

The receipt image is drawn by the open-source html2canvas library, loaded from jsDelivr only when you press the button. If it cannot load — offline, or blocked by your network — ZakatDay sends the text summary instead.

Accuracy & sources

Is this a fatwa? expand_more

No. ZakatDay is an arithmetic tool for the widely used thresholds and the 2.5% rate. Rules on particular assets — pensions, business inventory, jewellery in regular use, long-term debt — differ between schools. Please confirm your final figure with a qualified scholar.

What is the source of the nisab figures? expand_more

87.48 g of gold (7.5 tola) and 612.36 g of silver (52.5 tola), the values used in the classical manuals and in the calculator this site previously shipped. The zakat rate is 2.5% (1/40).

Is there an app to install? expand_more

ZakatDay is a web app that works in any modern browser. You can add it to your home screen from the browser menu for a full-screen experience, and the calculator keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
