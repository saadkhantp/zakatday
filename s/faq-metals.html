Gold & silver

Why do karat and weight both matter? expand_more

Market prices quote fine (24K) gold. A 22K ring weighing 10 g contains 9.17 g of pure gold, so its zakatable value is 9.17 g × the 24K price. ZakatDay converts grams, tola and troy ounces to fine metal for you, then multiplies by the live price per gram.

  • 24K = 999 purity, 22K = 916, 21K = 875, 18K = 750, 14K = 585
  • 1 tola = 11.6638 g, 1 troy ounce = 31.1034768 g
Is jewellery worn daily still zakatable? expand_more

The schools differ: the Hanafi position is that gold and silver jewellery is zakatable regardless of use, while other schools exempt jewellery in regular wear. ZakatDay treats all entered metal as zakatable — follow your school.

Where do the live prices come from? expand_more

Currency and metal rates come from public feeds (jsDelivr's currency-api, with open.er-api.com as a fallback). The app converts the USD spot price per troy ounce into your currency per gram.

Retail prices in your city usually differ because of making charges and local premiums — use Edit on the calculator to enter the price you are actually quoted.
