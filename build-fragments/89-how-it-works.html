How ZakatDay works

ZakatDay is a free, ad-free Zakat calculator for Muslims in the US, UK, Pakistan, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Malaysia, Canada, India, Australia and the EU. Enter your wealth in your own currency and it works out 2.5% of your net zakatable assets — entirely on your device.

Nisab in grams and tolas
Zakat becomes due once your wealth reaches the nisab: 612.36 g of silver (52.5 tola) or 87.48 g of gold (7.5 tola). ZakatDay multiplies that weight by the live price per gram of the metal you pick, so the threshold updates with the market instead of a fixed figure.
2.5% over one lunar year
The zakat rate is 2.5% (1/40) of the wealth you have held for a full hawl — one lunar year. The calculator applies that rate to your net assets, after debts.
Karat, tola and troy ounce support
Market prices quote fine 24K gold, so a 22K item weighing 10 g holds only 9.17 g of pure gold. Enter jewellery by weight in grams, tola or troy ounces and choose 14K, 18K, 21K, 22K or 24K — ZakatDay converts to fine metal before valuing it.
150+ currencies, live rates
Work in USD, GBP, EUR, PKR, INR, AED, SAR, MYR, CAD, AUD and more than 140 other currencies. Switching currency converts your amounts with live cross-rates rather than resetting what you typed.
Zero storage, zero sign-up
No account, no cookies carrying your figures, no database. Amounts live only in this tab's memory and disappear when you close it. Preferred standard and currency are the only saved settings.
